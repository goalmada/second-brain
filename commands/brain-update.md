---
description: Pull the latest curated practices pack into your brain
---

Update the shared practices pack without touching the user's own notes.

Steps:
1. In `~/.claude/brain`, fetch the latest `practices/` from the Second Brain repo.
2. Merge new or changed practice files into `~/.claude/brain/practices/`.
3. Never overwrite the user's `projects/`, `references/`, `meetings/`, `decisions/`, or `staging/`.
4. Report which practices were added or changed.
